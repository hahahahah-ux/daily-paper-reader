## 摘要
在富接触操作过程中，机器人与其环境之间的交互携带着局部几何信息：表面阻止穿透，孔引导销钉。利用这些交互的控制器能够在遵守环境约束的同时保持任务意图。机器人学习系统通常使用位置控制、力-位置混合控制或笛卡尔阻抗控制。其预先规定的跟踪目标、刚度或力控制方向可能无法匹配局部约束，并可能降低性能。我们在仿真中基于三种简单的交互原语学习了一种本体感受反射策略：弹簧、平面和导轨。该策略利用状态历史将任务空间命令映射为关节位置目标，无需直接的力或几何测量。训练完成后，它作为上层控制器之下的冻结执行层。我们在双臂箱体举升、销钉插入和表面跟随中对其进行了评估。在箱体举升中，反射策略将力保持在阈值以下，而基线方法则失败。在粗糙表面跟随中，它保持在 10 N 参考值以下，与经过调参的力-位置混合控制相当。在 0.02 mm 的销钉插入中，它将平均估计接触力降至基线的一半以下，同时将硬件成功率从至多 22% 提高到 36–58%。通过将接触响应与命令生成分离，该反射策略为运动规划器、学习策略和遥操作器提供了稳健的富接触执行能力。

## Abstract
During contact-rich manipulation, interactions between a robot and its environment carry information about local geometry: a surface prevents penetration, a bore guides a peg. A controller that exploits these interactions can comply with environmental constraints while preserving task intent. Robot-earning systems commonly use position, hybrid force-position, or Cartesian impedance control. Their prescribed tracking objectives, stiffness, or force-control directions may not match local constraints and may degrade performance. We learn a proprioceptive reflex policy in simulation on three simple interaction primitives: a spring, a plane, and a rail. The policy maps task-space commands to joint-position targets using state history, without direct force or geometrical measurements. Once trained, it serves as a frozen execution layer beneath higher-level controllers. We evaluate it in dual-arm box lifting, peg insertion, and surface following. In box lifting, the reflex kept the force below the threshold while the baseline failed. In rough-surface following it stayed below the 10 N reference, on par with tuned hybrid force-position control. In 0.02 mm peg insertion It reduced mean estimated contact force to less than half that of the baseline while increasing hardware success rates from at most 22 % to 36-58 %. By separating contact response from command generation, the reflex policy provides motion planners, learned policies, and teleoperators with robust contact-rich execution.
