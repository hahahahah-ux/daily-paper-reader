## 摘要
物理主动视觉使机器人能够在任务相关观测变得不可靠时改变其视角，但现有的操作基准在支持研究策略如何在执行过程中从遮挡中恢复方面仍很有限。我们提出了 BAVO-Bench（Bimanual Active Vision under Occlusion，遮挡下的双臂主动视觉），一个双臂主动视觉基准，通过 Clean、Stage Occlusion 和 Random-time Occlusion 条件系统地控制外部可见性，从而能够评估操作性能和主动视觉恢复。基于这一设置，我们提出了 A-FAR（Active Future-Aware Recovery，主动未来感知恢复），一种用于视角与操作联合控制的主动视觉策略。A-FAR 将移动相机观测表示在统一的以机器人为中心的三维坐标系中，并从预训练的 4D 模型中蒸馏关系结构及其未来演化，从而为策略提供未来感知的几何引导，而无需在部署时获取未来观测。在多个操作任务上的实验表明，A-FAR 提高了对结构化遮挡和时间偏移遮挡的鲁棒性，同时在干净观测下保持强劲性能。

## Abstract
Physical active vision allows robots to change their viewpoint when task-relevant observations become unreliable, yet existing manipulation benchmarks provide limited support for studying how policies recover from occlusion during execution. We introduce BAVO-Bench (Bimanual Active Vision under Occlusion), a bimanual active-vision benchmark that systematically controls external visibility through Clean, Stage Occlusion, and Random-time Occlusion conditions, enabling evaluation of both manipulation performance and active visual recovery. Building on this setting, we present A-FAR (Active Future-Aware Recovery), an active-vision policy for joint viewpoint and manipulation control. A-FAR represents moving-camera observations in a unified robot-centric 3D frame and distills relational structure together with its future evolution from a pretrained 4D model, providing the policy with future-aware geometric guidance without requiring future observations at deployment. Experiments across multiple manipulation tasks show that A-FAR improves robustness to both structured and temporally shifted occlusions while maintaining strong performance under clean observations.
