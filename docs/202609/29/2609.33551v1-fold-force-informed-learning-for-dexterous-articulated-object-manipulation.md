## 摘要
将人类演示迁移到灵巧机器人仍然具有挑战性，因为手部形态和接触动力学的差异常常导致重定向后的动作无法产生预期的物体行为。我们提出 FoLD，一个通过显式力引导学习灵巧操作铰接物体的框架。FoLD 根据人类演示以及机器人当前交互状态计算补偿力场，从而产生一种促进所演示物体运动的力先验。该力先验为残差策略提供信息，使重定向后的手部动作适应任务的接触需求。我们在一个公开的铰接物体操作基准上评估 FoLD，它在不同任务和不同本体上持续优于最先进的基线。我们进一步在真实灵巧机器人平台上验证 FoLD，展示了将人类操作技能成功迁移到机器人执行。项目页面链接：https://gghgghgghgg.github.io/FoLD-project-page/。

## Abstract
Transferring human demonstrations to dexterous robots remains challenging because differences in hand morphology and contact dynamics often cause retargeted motions to fail at producing the intended object behavior. We present \textbf{FoLD}, a framework for learning dexterous manipulation of articulated objects through explicit force guidance. FoLD compute compensatory force fields from human demonstrations together with the robot's current interaction state, yielding a force prior that promotes the demonstrated object motion. This force prior informs a residual policy that adapts retargeted hand motions to the contact requirements of the task. We evaluate FoLD on a public benchmark for articulated object manipulation, where it consistently outperforms state-of-the-art baselines across tasks and embodiments. We further validate FoLD on real dexterous robot platforms, demonstrating successful transfer of human manipulation skills to robot execution.
